How do physics principles explain guitars?

Lee, Jacob; Rendich, Isaac

Viewbank College

Introduction

In this physics project, we sought to investigate the implications of physics principles on sound and more specifically - instruments. Whilst instruments involving sound are generally recreational, there are real world, socioeconomic results that are based off of scientific research and understanding the of sound.

Motivations

We chose this topic as both of us are guitar and music enthusiasts. Music forms a lot of our daily lives and we were interested in the origin of sounds produced by the instruments that music is made of. Understanding the core concepts of music enable manufacturers to develop accessible software for music production as well as further instruments, which is powerful in a socioeconomic context as it may enable cost effective music producion alternatives for people who are unable to fund expensive, high-end equipment.

The implications of further research

Further research into sound may provide surprising insights into the nature of both our interpretations of sound as humans, as well as the means it is produced and factors which influence its quality. The medical technology ultrasound is based off of high-frequency sounds, demonstrating that sound research is not just limited to recreation, and can have other - incredibly relevant and real world effects.
