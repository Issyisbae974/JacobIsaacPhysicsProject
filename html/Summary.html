4.1 - How do physics principles explain guitars?

The described physics concepts, pitch and timbre in addition to other properties are what enable manufacturers to develop guitars and other instruments. Pitch dictates the Western tuning system, and enables chords and harmonies to follow the natural harmonic series, which is pleasant to human ears. Timbre enables the utility of instruments with differing qualities, the difference between a guitar and a piano, or a trumpet. Together, these fundamental ideas form the basis of music, and are crucial in the initial development and innovation of further instruments, even digital instruments built using synthesizers are based off of these fundamental ideas surrounding pitch and timbre.
