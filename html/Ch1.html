1.1 - What is sound?

Sound is mechanical energy, "Sound is a pressure disturbance that moves through a medium in the form of mechanical waves" (Iowa State University, 2021). In our case when a string is plucked, it vibrates and moves particles in the atmosphere through creating pressure disturbances.

(Flipping Physics, n.d.)

1.2 - What is pitch?

Pitch is the human ears interpretation of different frequencies of sound and is given the unit of hertz (cycles per second). Every sound has pitch, whether it be a fly buzzing around, a car screeching to a stop or a door slamming shut. However, most sounds are made up of multiple different pitches all expressed at once. The dominantly identified pitch from the sound source is named the fundamental frequency.
